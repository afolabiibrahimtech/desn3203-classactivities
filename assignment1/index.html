Cristiano Ronaldo

Portuguese footballer | Born February 5, 1985 | Funchal, Madeira

Who is Cristiano Ronaldo?

Cristiano Ronaldo is a forward who plays for Al-Nassr in Saudi Arabia and captains the Portugal national team. Most fans know him as CR7, after his initials and his shirt number. He has won the Ballon d'Or five times and over 30 trophies in his career, and he is the top goalscorer in the history of men's football. I picked him because no other player I know has stayed at the top for this long. (Wikipedia, 2026)


Early Life

Ronaldo was the youngest of four children and grew up in a small, poor home in Funchal, sharing a room with his brother and sisters. His mother was a cook and his father was a gardener who also helped out with the kits at a local team called Andorinha, which is where Ronaldo started playing. At just 12 years old he left his family and moved to Lisbon to train at the Sporting CP academy. (Wikipedia, 2026)

Manchester United

After Sporting beat Manchester United in a 2003 friendly, Sir Alex Ferguson signed the 18-year-old for 12 million pounds and gave him the number 7 shirt. At first he was a skinny winger who loved tricks, but over six seasons he turned into a goal machine. He helped United win three Premier League titles in a row and the 2008 Champions League, and that year he won his first Ballon d'Or. (Wikipedia, 2026)


Real Madrid

In 2009 Real Madrid bought Ronaldo for 80 million pounds, which was a world record at the time. His nine years in Spain were the best of his career. He scored 450 goals in 438 games, more than anyone in the club's history, and won four Champions League titles between 2014 and 2018. He also won four more Ballon d'Or awards while playing in Madrid. (Wikipedia, 2026)

Later Career and Portugal

Ronaldo moved to Juventus in 2018 and won Serie A twice, then had a short second spell at Manchester United before joining Al-Nassr in 2023. In 2026 he helped Al-Nassr win the Saudi league title. For Portugal, he led the team to its first ever major trophy at Euro 2016, and at the 2026 World Cup he became the first player to score at six different World Cups. (Wikipedia, 2026)
